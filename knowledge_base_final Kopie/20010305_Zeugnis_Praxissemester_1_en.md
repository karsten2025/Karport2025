## Certificate

Mr. Karsten Zenk carried out, in the summer semester 2000 at the Institute for Manufacturing Processes and Testing as part of the practical semester, hardness measurements on screw and kneading elements as well as a literature review on the topic of wear on screw elements of twin-screw plastic extruders for the company Theysohn Maschinenbau.

The hardness measurements had the scope of a minor study project and required a CAD-assisted design of suitable fixtures for measuring the screw and kneading elements.

The literature review had the scope of a major study project, for which the procurement of external literature, in part from international libraries, was required.

Mr. Zenk completed this work—which can be used in the marketing of Theysohn Maschinenbau for customer meetings—with care, determination, and success, and proved to be resilient.

As a future mechanical engineer, Mr. Zenk can certainly be deployed in a versatile manner with success.

Prof. Knipfelberg
