## Certificate of Participation

## Mr. Karsten Zenk

participated on 14 May 2008 in Salzgitter in the half-day seminar organized by the Legal Department of Salzgitter AG

## Fundamentals of German Contract for Work and Services Law (Werkvertragsrecht)

The seminar essentially covered the following topics:

- Overview and differentiation of common contract types
- Principal performance obligations of the parties to a contract for work and services
- Typical lifecycle of a project under a contract for work and services
- Distinction between unit-price contract and lump-sum fixed-price contract
- Forms of remuneration and securing claims
- Overview of acceptance options (Abnahme)
- Overview of performance disruptions / breaches of performance obligations
- Liability for defects in contract for work and services law before/after acceptance
- Early termination of the contract for work and services
- Causes and consequences of change orders (Nachträge) in contracts for work and services
- Distinction and differences between a German Civil Code (BGB) contract for work and services and a contract for work and services under VOB/B
- Required contract contents in contract drafting
