## Mr. Karsten Zenk

participated in a continuing education group to acquire consulting competence in Transactional Analysis from 13/09/2007 to 28/06/2008.

The continuing education period comprised the required scope of 120 training hours. During this time, foundational knowledge of the following concepts was taught:

- Ego states and diagnoses according to E. Berne
- Contaminations
- Triangular contracts according to F. English
- Ego states and role concepts according to B. Schmid
- Racket system according to Erskine and Zalcman
- Miniscript according to Taibi Kahler
- Impasse of the 1st and 2nd order
- Character types (over-, under-secure) according to F. English
- Redefinition concepts according to Kiltz and Elbing
