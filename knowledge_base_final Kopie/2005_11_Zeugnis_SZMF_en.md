## REFERENCE

Mr. Dipl.-Ing. Karsten Z e n k, born on 07 September 1967 in Wolfenbüttel, joined Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH on 01 April 2002 as a Project Engineer.

Mr. Zenk initially worked until December 2003 in Application Development within the Simulation and Engineering Department of the Materials Center, where he was independently entrusted primarily with the following tasks:

- Planning and execution of internationally funded research projects with responsibility for costs and schedules, coordinating Group-wide participation
- Participation in the planning and execution of automotive lightweight construction projects with customers regarding the further processing of steel (e.g., lightweight steel solutions through the use of higher-strength steels, use of innovative manufacturing processes such as roll forming and IHU)
- Preparation and calculation of linear and nonlinear finite element analyses using the CAD software CATIA and the FEM analysis software ANSYS and LS-Dyna3D

In connection with the consolidation of the research activities of Mannesmann Forschungsinstitut GmbH in Duisburg and parts of the Materials Center of Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH in Salzgitter, Salzgitter Mannesmann Forschung GmbH was founded in January 2004. All research and development activities of the Salzgitter Group are concentrated within it.

Since then, Mr. Zenk has been assigned to the Forming Technology Department and tasked with the following responsibilities:

- Application and forming-technology customer consulting
  - Support in material selection as well as in component design suitable for the material and manufacturing process
  - Supervision of material sample processing
  - Optimization of material usage and forming processes at the customer
- Representation of the company’s interests in national and international technical associations and committees
- Problem analysis and resolution of forming-technology issues for customers of Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH
- Close cooperation with research institutions; initiation and execution of internal and external development projects
- Supervision and guidance of students in the preparation of term papers and diploma theses

In addition to his work as a Project Engineer, Mr. Zenk, as Quality Management Representative of Salzgitter Mannesmann Forschung GmbH, was responsible for establishing, successfully certifying, and maintaining a quality and project management system in accordance with the requirements of DIN ISO 9001 and ISO/TS 16949 as a “remote location”.

Mr. Zenk has sound specialist expertise and handled even difficult tasks confidently and independently. Furthermore, he possesses extensive interdisciplinary knowledge, which he implemented excellently in his work. Issues of higher complexity were resolved very successfully by Mr. Zenk, among other things through making use of the training opportunities offered.

Mr. Zenk’s working style was characterized by a high degree of independence, exemplary reliability, and a high level of commitment. He was resilient and met the specified deadlines even under exceptional workload. In doing so, Mr. Zenk consistently demonstrated a high degree of flexibility in his thinking and actions. Good conceptual skills, very good analytical skills, and a very quick grasp of matters distinguished him.

His determination and willingness to perform were exemplary. Mr. Zenk was open, worked very cooperatively, and brought conflict situations to excellent outcomes.

He consistently lived up to his leadership responsibility as a Project Manager and fulfilled the task assigned to him—leading team members in a goal-, performance-, and team-oriented manner—always to our fullest satisfaction. His conduct toward superiors, colleagues, and employees was always exemplary. He was also highly valued by our customers, not least due to his courteous and professional manner.

Mr. Zenk is leaving our company today at his own request. We very much regret this decision, as we are losing a valuable future management talent. We thank him for the trusting cooperation and wish him continued professional success and all the best personally.
