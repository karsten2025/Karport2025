## EMPLOYMENT REFERENCE

Mr. Karsten Zenk, born 07/09/1967, residing at Am Roten Amte 2, 38302 Wolfenbüttel, was employed in our company from 01/02/1996 to 15/09/1997 as an employee in Quality Management.

As part of a work placement, he acquired knowledge from January 1994 to April 1994 in quality management activities, production planning, project management work, and work organization within the framework of Lean Production.

His areas of responsibility included the following activities:

- Monitoring quality assurance tasks in production, such as compliance with production sequences according to the production plan, compliance with process parameters, and performing 100% inspections
- Recording rework and scrap, as well as customer visits resulting from these issues
- Performing and documenting sample inspections
- Conducting series measurements as a basis for capability studies
- Evaluating inspection results using statistical methods
- Blocking production batches in the event of deviations from target specifications
- Acting as a substitute in incoming goods inspection
- Managing the quarantine/blocked stock warehouse

Mr. Zenk actively contributed to preparing our company for certification in accordance with DIN EN ISO 9001. In doing so, he acquired sound knowledge of the standard, particularly in the area of complaint handling.

During his employment with our company, in addition to his consistently very active contribution, he also demonstrated a strong interest in external continuing education. Among other things, he attended a seminar on Statistical Process Control (SPC) and successfully completed the EC contact-study program at the University of Applied Sciences Braunschweig/Wolfenbüttel in the field of Logistics and Production Management.

As part of educational leave, he acquired knowledge in the use of the spreadsheet software EXCEL 5.0 in controlling.

We have come to know Mr. Zenk as an independent, self-reliant employee.

He always performed the tasks assigned to him to our fullest satisfaction. His conduct towards supervisors and colleagues was always impeccable.

Mr. Zenk is leaving us on 15/09/1997 at his own request.

We wish him every success in his further career.
