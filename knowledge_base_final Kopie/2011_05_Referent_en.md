## Activities as a Lecturer at Our Organization

Mr. Karsten Zenk, residing at Ferdinandstr. 14, 38300 Wolfenbüttel, works as an instructor in our organization in the Industrial Master (Industriemeister) training program for the subject Quality Management (QM) / Statistics.

Mr. Zenk delivered the training in accordance with the requirements of the framework curriculum for Industrial Masters in Metallurgical Plant Technology (Industriemeister Hüttentechnik).

The scope of instruction amounted to approximately 24 teaching hours per master course. In addition, each course included exam preparation in the form of educational leave (Bildungsurlaub), during which Mr. Zenk provided additional support with 2 teaching hours in the subject QM / Statistics.

Mr. Zenk performed this work to our fullest satisfaction.

We would be pleased if Mr. Zenk can continue to work for us in the future and support us in the training of Industrial Masters.
