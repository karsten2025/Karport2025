## QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS AND INTERNAL AUDIT

During the course, knowledge was imparted on the selection, implementation, documentation, and evaluation of quality management systems.

The following topics were covered:

- Development and current significance of quality management
- Quality planning and quality control
- Introduction to the ISO 9000 family
- From text analysis to the audit question
- Planning, conducting, and follow-up of internal audits
- Documentation of a quality management system
- Implementation of a quality management system
- Maintenance of a quality management system
- Legal aspects
- Facilitation, visualization, presentation

The course content was deepened through discussion and group work using case studies.
