## **Agile Metrics for Success**

Issued by the Project Management Institute

Earners of this badge completed the eLearning course and assessment on how to select effective metrics aligned with team and organizational goals. Earners learned how to avoid misleading and counterproductive metrics and how to replace them with metrics that promote collaboration and information sharing.
