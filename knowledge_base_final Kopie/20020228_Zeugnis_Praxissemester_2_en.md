## **Internship Certificate**

Mr. Karsten Zenk completed his 2nd practical semester internship at Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH in the TQA-B department from 16/07/2001 to 28/02/2002. During this period, Mr. Zenk prepared a literature review as part of a small academic project, presenting the current state of the art in bending technology for tubes and profiles. Furthermore, in his diploma thesis he developed a fully parameterized FEM simulation model for pre-bending tubes for subsequent internal high-pressure forming.

The tasks assigned to Mr. Zenk during the internship were always carried out to our fullest satisfaction. For all employees, the internship period meant a pleasant collaboration with Mr. Zenk.

His conduct toward supervisors and colleagues was always impeccable. We wish Mr. Zenk all the best for his future professional and personal path.
