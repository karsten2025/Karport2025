About Me

1. Perspective & Value: The Systems Engineer

I approach organizations as complex adaptive systems. Rather than managing rigid machines, I engineer robust architectures that render complexity manageable. Together, we identify the structural levers that lower transaction costs and operationalize strategic goals.

2. Methodology & Culture: The Pragmatist

I generate real change through functional incentive structures, not persuasion. I work with the system’s inherent dynamics. Thus, we cultivate an environment where resistance serves not as a blockade, but as a valuable data signal for necessary process adjustments.

3. Interfaces & Translation: The Interface Manager

I focus on bridging the gap between technology, business, and the customer. Through precise translation, I eliminate misunderstandings as the most expensive form of waste. I leverage diverse perspectives to transform friction into technical and strategic innovation.

4. Background & Execution: The Grounded Practitioner

From mechanical engineering, I bring a deep grasp of functional coherence—from component to system architecture. I combine this grounded experience with modern management theory. When strategy meets reality, I ensure systemic calm and execution discipline to guarantee operability.

Foundation & Standards

I combine the craftsmanship of a Graduate Engineer (Dipl.-Ing.) with internationally recognized standards from the Project Management Institute (PMI). My methodological toolkit is hybrid: Ranging from classical large-scale project control (PMP®) and agile complexity management (PMI-ACP®) to establishing strategic Value Management Offices (PMOCP™). I actively integrate AI expertise (CPMAI™) into project work to render technological disruption manageable.
