Project: Transforming Pragmatism into Technical Precision

Period: 1984 – 1991 | Portfolio: Operational Excellence | Program: Manufacturing Efficiency Enhancement

Objective (O)

To master the entire value creation process from manual machining to automated production to increase productivity and quality.

Key Results (KRs)

  • Reduce cycle times on CNC machines by 15% through independent programming and process optimization.
  • Ensure a scrap rate of less than 2% on all independently produced parts.
  • Actively contribute to 3+ innovative product developments.

Value Proposition

Internal Perspective (Company)

KPIs: Reduced Cycle Time; Increased Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE); Reduced Cost of Poor Quality.

External Perspective (Customer)

KPIs: Increased Product Quality and Precision; Ensured On-Time Delivery.
