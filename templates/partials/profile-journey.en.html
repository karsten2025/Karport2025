The Foundation: Pragmatism & Mechanical Precision (1967 – 2002)

Growing up in a family business environment (construction sector), process thinking and operational responsibility were lived reality from an early age. An apprenticeship as a machine fitter (EDER-Maschinenbau, 1984–1991) with independent CNC programming sharpened the understanding of manufacturing precision and value stream design.

Advanced training as a state-certified Machine Technician (Automation Technology, Teutloff 1991–1993) and Quality Assurance Technician (Teutloff 1994) established the technical-methodological foundation. Early professional experience in production planning (WILKE GmbH), quality management (GURTEC / SANDVIK Group), and team leadership (Parquet Studio Vattersen) completed the profile of a practitioner who understands, designs, and improves systems.

A Mechanical Engineering degree (FH, 1997–2002) with a focus on Production & Logistics – culminating in a diploma thesis at Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH (FEM simulation, tube bending) – was the logical synthesis of deep technical knowledge and direct industrial applicability.
