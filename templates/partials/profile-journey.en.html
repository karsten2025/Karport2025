Professional Journey

My career path is marked by a continuous evolution from a technical expert to a strategic architect of complex transformation processes. For over decades, I have learned how to achieve sustainable results in demanding industrial environments by aligning technology, processes, and people.

Value Creation Through Versatile Project Management in Heavy Industry (2007 – today)

In my long-term role with a leading German steel manufacturer, I apply my passion for coalescing ideas and delivering tangible outcomes within complex socio-technical systems. As a proactive and situational executor, I flexibly adapt my approach—be it predictive, adaptive, iterative, or hybrid—to meet the specific demands of each project. My strength lies in stakeholder-centric communication, which enables me to successfully navigate diverse teams with nuanced differences and lead projects to success. My contribution to the company's value creation is diverse and demonstrated through a series of successfully completed major projects that target various corporate goals: Increasing Cost-Efficiency: Through the design and installation of a fully automated crane system with integrated information logistics , as well as the introduction of hot boxes and optimized logistics processes. Sustainability and Environmental Compliance: Through the integration of a new wastewater pre-treatment facility and a process gas purification system to meet environmental standards. Safety and Infrastructure: By leading sub-projects in major repairs and overhauling a sintering plant's ignition hood to meet rigorous safety standards. Strategic Business Development: By formulating a compelling business case for utilizing steel by-products, which influenced key project decisions. Currently, I am leveraging this comprehensive experience as an associated project manager in one of the company's central transformation projects.

Investing in Resilience: A Deliberate Prioritization (2007)

Professional success is sustainable when one's personal foundation is stable. During this phase, I made the conscious decision to devote my full attention and energy to my family. This period was not a break, but a valuable investment in long-term resilience, clarity, and balance. Strengthening this foundation makes it possible to navigate complex professional challenges with empathy, focus, and sustained energy, and it underscores my conviction that the human being is at the center of every successful system.

Value Creation in a Medium-Sized Business: Process Reorganization and Strategic Realignment (2005 – 2007)

As a project engineer with cross-functional responsibilities at Wilhelm Hebenstreit GmbH, I took on a central role in the structural development of the company. My task was to unlock potential and position the company for the future. I analyzed, modeled, and reorganized business processes in coordination with those responsible to increase efficiency. In purchasing, I conducted potential analyses, negotiated with suppliers, and thereby optimized procurement costs. As the representative of the operations manager, I took responsibility for order acceptance, capacity planning, and schedule monitoring. Furthermore, I managed mechanical engineering projects in special-purpose machinery and represented the company in cooperation initiatives aimed at sustainable cost reduction. My ability to lead teams in a goal-, performance-, quality-, and cost-oriented manner was rated by the company as "to our fullest satisfaction." Through solution-oriented ideas and by taking full responsibility, I made a significant contribution to increasing the company's value at both the operational and strategic levels.

Structural Value Creation: From Research to a Certified Management System (2002 – 2005)

As a project engineer at Salzgitter Mannesmann Forschung GmbH, I contributed my skills in two key areas: leading innovative international research projects and establishing certified management systems. I held cost and deadline responsibility for international research projects and advised automotive clients on the implementation of lightweighting solutions through the use of higher-strength steels and innovative manufacturing processes like hydroforming. In doing so, I utilized complex Finite Element calculations to optimize material use and forming processes at the customer's site. In parallel, as the Quality Management Representative, I was responsible for the development, successful certification, and maintenance of a quality and project management system in accordance with the demanding DIN ISO 9001 and ISO/TS 16949 standards. My leadership as a project manager was rated as "always to our fullest satisfaction". The company extraordinarily regretted my departure, stating they were losing a "valuable junior executive", which underscores the value I created.

From Knowledge to Value Creation: Academic Excellence Meets Industrial Practice (1997 – 2002)

For me, studying mechanical engineering with a focus on production and logistics was the logical next step to combine theoretical depth with application-oriented problem-solving. Going far beyond the curriculum, I actively sought collaboration with industry to transform academic knowledge directly into measurable value. For the company Theysohn Maschinenbau, I conducted complex hardness measurements and an international literature review on the subject of wear, the results of which were directly used in the company's marketing and customer discussions. The culmination was my diploma thesis, conducted at Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH and graded as "very good". I developed a fully parameterized FEM simulation model for tube bending, a challenging task in the field of hydroforming. The company certified that all assigned tasks were "always carried out to their fullest satisfaction". These projects demonstrate my ability to apply complex scientific methods in a targeted manner to create relevant, directly usable, and value-adding solutions for companies.

Creating Value Through Applied Quality (1996 – 1997)

Upon my return to WILKE as a quality management employee, I directly translated theory into practice to increase customer satisfaction and process efficiency. My role was to proactively shape quality: I monitored quality assurance measures in production, conducted serial measurements as a basis for statistical process control (SPC), and evaluated test results using statistical methods. By consistently recording rework and scrap and blocking non-conforming production lots, I was able to directly contribute to cost reduction and the stabilization of serial processes. A key success was my active participation in preparing the company for its DIN EN ISO 9001 certification. The company rated my independent work style and results as "always to their fullest satisfaction".

Value Stream Design and Quality Management (1995 – 1996)

At GURTEC, a SANDVIK Group company, I was able to immediately apply my newly acquired qualifications in a larger corporate context. My main responsibility was the value stream-oriented planning, orchestrating, and overseeing of deliveries and services in close collaboration with the production team to enhance the production flow. Furthermore, I played a pivotal role in devising a comprehensive quality management system, allowing me to directly apply my certified knowledge to the company's transformative initiatives. Through this dual focus on value stream efficiency and systematic quality assurance, I helped establish leaner, more reliable processes and embed a sustainable culture of quality.

Leadership in Practice: Amplifying Productivity in a Skilled Trade (1994 – 1995)

During the transition phase into my professional career, I seized the opportunity to immediately apply my management skills at Parquet studio Vattersen. I transferred my experience from my parents' business to this new environment, taking on responsibility for managing construction industry orders and leading small, self-organized teams. By applying these field-tested methods, we were able to achieve a tangible increase in productivity. Quality as a Strategic Focus: The Specialization (1994)

My practical experience in process optimization at Wilke showed me unequivocally that quality is the decisive lever for sustainable business success. Driven by this insight, I proactively chose to pursue targeted advanced training to become a state-certified quality assurance technician at the Teutloff Academy. This specialization was a deliberate investment to acquire the in-depth knowledge needed to systematically plan, control, and assure quality. The goal was not just to correct errors, but to create robust processes that reduce waste, increase customer satisfaction, and thus directly enhance company value.

Immediately after my technical qualification, I put my knowledge to the test in the production planning department at WILKE GmbH. There, I transformed theory into measurable results by creating and reviewing sequence plans for work steps and actively co-designing workplaces in collaboration with the work organization department. In coordination with quality assurance, I assessed components and evaluated their manufacturing possibilities to optimize processes across various areas—from plastics processing to tool and fixture construction. This work, which the company rated as "always to our complete satisfaction," was the first proof that my systemic approach to designing workflows creates real business value.

Developing Systems Intelligence: A Human-Centric Approach to Automation (1991 – 1993)

The realization that true value creation lies in the intelligent connection of processes motivated me to pursue advanced training to become a state-certified Machine Technician specializing in Automation Technology. My focus was on understanding how technology can expand human potential rather than merely replacing it. This education at the Teutloff Academy was a decisive step from pure execution to the conception and design of socio-technical systems. It created the theoretical foundation for leading complex automation projects that not only increase efficiency but also place people at the center, thereby creating sustainable, high-performance work environments.

Building on my pragmatic foundation, I deliberately deepened my skills during my apprenticeship as a machine fitter at EDER-Maschinenbau. In this innovative small company, I learned not only to shape metal with precision but to master the entire value creation process. Through the independent programming and optimization the function of CNC machines, I significantly contributed to reducing cycle times, which boosted productivity while maintaining a consistently high level of quality. Actively participating in innovative product developments sharpened my understanding of how technical expertise translates directly into measurable business success. This period laid the groundwork for my ability not just to execute projects, but to design and optimize them for maximum value creation.
