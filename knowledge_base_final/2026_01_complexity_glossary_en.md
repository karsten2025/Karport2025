# Complexity Primer — Glossary (Operational)

This compact glossary is intended to standardize terminology for chatbot responses and KB retrieval.

- Emergence: Macro-level properties or behaviors that arise from local interactions among parts; may be weak (explainable) or strong (not easily deducible).
- Agent-Based Model (ABM): Computational simulation of heterogeneous agents interacting via local rules, used to explore mechanisms and counterfactuals.
- Diversity: Differences in types or attributes among agents. Supports exploration and robustness, but may require coordination.
- Explore / Exploit: Trade-off between searching for better options (explore) and using known good ones (exploit).
- Rugged Landscape: Optimization landscape with many local optima; requires exploratory search.
- Dancing Landscape: Landscape whose optima shift over time as agents adapt; requires continuous exploration and adaptation.
- Self-Organized Criticality: Regime where systems produce cascades/avalanches with heavy-tailed size distributions.
- Tipping Point / Phase Transition: A threshold beyond which a system undergoes abrupt change.
- Path Dependence: Early events shape long-term trajectories; small differences can be amplified.
- Nonstationarity: Statistical properties change over time; past data may not predict future behavior.

Usage: Prefer these operational definitions when answering user queries.
