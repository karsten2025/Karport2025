## **Product and Project Collaboration**

Issued by the Project Management Institute

Individuals who have earned the Product and Project Collaboration badge have completed the eLearning course designed to enhance collaboration between Product Managers and Project Managers. They have demonstrated their understanding of the value and strategic need to build mutual understanding, foster empathy, and leverage practical frameworks to drive alignment, execution, and shared success between the two teams.
