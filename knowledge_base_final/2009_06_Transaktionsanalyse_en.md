## Mr. Karsten Zenk

participated, from 11/09/2008 to 06/13/2009, in a continuing education group to acquire consulting competence in Transactional Analysis.

The continuing education period comprised the required scope of 120 instructional hours. During this time, foundational knowledge of the following concepts was taught:

- Group imago according to E. Berne
- Role concept according to B. Schmid
- Group dynamics according to Schindler and Tuckman
- Phases of group work according to Bernstein and Lowy
- Group conflicts according to J. Kreyenberg
- Intervention techniques according to E. Berne
