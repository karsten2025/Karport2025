## **Benefits Realization Through Versatile Project Management in Heavy Industry (2007 – Present)**

In my many years of work for a leading German steel manufacturer, I bring my passion for turning ideas into tangible results within complex socio-technical systems. As a situational and proactive project manager, I flexibly adapt my approach to the respective requirements—whether predictive, adaptive, iterative, or hybrid. My strength lies in stakeholder-centered communication, which enables me to successfully navigate even multifaceted teams with differing interests and to lead projects to success. My contribution to the company’s value creation is diverse and is reflected in a series of successfully delivered large-scale projects that support different corporate objectives: Increasing cost efficiency: through the design and installation of a fully automated crane system with integrated information logistics, as well as through the introduction of heat-protection hoods and optimized logistics processes. Sustainability and environmental compliance: through the integration of a new wastewater pre-treatment plant as well as a process gas cleaning system to meet environmental standards. Safety and infrastructure: through leading subprojects during major repairs and the refurbishment of the ignition hood of a sinter plant to meet the strictest safety standards. Strategic business development: through the development of a business case for the utilization of steel by-products, which significantly influenced key project decisions. I currently contribute this extensive experience as an associate project manager in a central transformation project within the company.

#### **Efficiency Gains in the SME Sector: Process Reorganization and Strategic Realignment (2005 – 2007)**

As a project engineer in a cross-functional role at Wilhelm Hebenstreit GmbH, I assumed a central role in the company’s structural development. My task was to unlock potential and position the company for the future. I analyzed, modeled, and reorganized business processes in coordination with those responsible in order to increase efficiency. In procurement, I conducted potential analyses, negotiated with suppliers, and thus optimized purchasing costs. As deputy to the operations manager, I took responsibility for order intake, capacity planning, and schedule monitoring. In addition, I led mechanical engineering projects in special-purpose machinery and represented the company in cooperative initiatives aimed at sustainable cost reduction. My ability to lead teams with a focus on objectives, performance, quality, and cost was assessed by the company as “to our fullest satisfaction.” Through solution-oriented ideas and by assuming full responsibility, I made a significant contribution to increasing the company’s value at both the operational and strategic levels.

# **From Knowledge to Unlocking Potential: Academic Excellence Meets Industrial Practice (1997 – 2002)**

Studying mechanical engineering with a focus on production and logistics was the logical next step for me to combine theoretical depth with application-oriented problem solving. Far beyond the curriculum, I actively sought collaboration with industry in order to translate academic knowledge directly into measurable value. For example, for Theysohn Maschinenbau I carried out complex hardness measurements and an international literature review on wear, the results of which fed directly into the company’s marketing and customer discussions. The highlight was my diploma thesis conducted at Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH, which was graded “very good.” I developed a fully parameterized FEM simulation model for tube bending—an advanced task in the field of internal high-pressure forming. The company certified that all assigned tasks were “always carried out to our fullest satisfaction.” These projects demonstrate my ability to apply complex scientific methods in a targeted manner to create solutions that are relevant to companies, directly usable, and value-enhancing.

#### **Value Creation Through Lived Quality (1996 – 1997)**

Upon my return to WILKE as an employee in quality management, I translated theory directly into practice to increase customer satisfaction and process efficiency. My task was to shape quality proactively: I monitored quality assurance measures in production, conducted series measurements as a basis for statistical process control (SPC), and evaluated test results using statistical methods. By consistently recording rework and scrap and blocking defective production batches, I was able to contribute directly to cost reduction and the stabilization of series production processes. A key success was my active involvement in preparing the company for certification to DIN EN ISO 9001. The company rated my independent way of working and the results as “always to our fullest satisfaction.”
