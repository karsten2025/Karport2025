Mr. Dipl.-Ing. Karsten Zenk, born on 07 September 1967, was employed in our company from 01/12/2005 to 01/06/2007 as a Project Engineer in a cross-functional role.

Within the scope of his employment with our company, he worked independently in the following areas of responsibility:

- Planning and execution of small and medium-sized mechanical engineering projects for our customers in special-purpose machinery and the metal decorating (sheet-metal printing) industry.
- Analysis, modelling, and reorganisation of business processes in coordination with the respective process owners.
- Initiation, planning, and implementation of development initiatives, including the recruitment, supervision, and guidance of students.
- Conceptualisation and development of the company’s external presentation through presentations and the website.
- Representation of the company’s interests in working groups of the Mechanical Engineering Cooperation Initiative in Braunschweig, with the objective of sustainable cost reductions.
- Potential analysis in procurement, including quotation negotiations, selection, and commissioning of suppliers.
- Technology assessments taking economic boundary conditions into account, and management of the resulting design- and manufacturing-related adaptation projects.
- Deputising for the Operations Manager, including the associated tasks of order acceptance, capacity and production planning, and schedule monitoring.

Mr. Zenk mastered his areas of work comprehensively and possessed outstanding specialist knowledge that was available at all times. In new situations, he found his way confidently and consistently developed very good solutions.
