## Certificate of Attendance

## Mr Karsten Zenk

attended the half-day seminar held on 10 June 2008 in Salzgitter, jointly organized by the Plant Procurement Department of Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH and the Legal Department of Salzgitter AG:

## Drafting of Contracts for Work and Services in Plant Engineering

The seminar essentially covered the following topics:

- Review of a complete standard plant engineering contract template
- Incorporation of the bill of quantities and/or the purchase specification into the contract for work and services to define the contractually owed work result
- Hierarchy of contractual bases and reference documents
- Remuneration provisions and advance payments under a lump-sum fixed-price contract
- Importance and stipulation of contractual deadlines
- Non-performance, delay, and fundamentals of contractual penalties for schedule overruns
- Acceptance and warranty
- Option rights, changes in scope of work, and change-order risks
- Internal rules of responsibility for contract amendments/change orders
