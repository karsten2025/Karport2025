# A Complexity Primer — Explore/Exploit & Emergence

## Explore vs Exploit
- Exploration: search for better options; Exploitation: use known good options.
- Strategies: simulated annealing (high 'temperature' → exploration; gradual cooling → exploitation).
- Practical rule: allocate stage-based resources for exploration, monitor results, scale successful experiments.

## Emergence
- Emergence occurs when local interactions generate macro-level structures or functions not evident from individual parts.
- Types: weak (explained from rules) vs strong (not directly deducible).

## Practical takeaways
1. Use simple conceptual models to surface mechanisms before building complex simulations.
2. Expect emergent outcomes; design monitoring and interventions that can react to unexpected macro behavior.
