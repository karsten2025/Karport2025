## **Appointment**

Mr. Karsten Zenk is hereby appointed, with immediate effect, as Quality Management Representative (QMR) of Salzgitter Mannesmann Forschung GmbH (SZMF), Salzgitter site.

His responsibilities include:

- implementing and further developing the quality objectives and adapting the QMS to changed conditions,
- maintaining, distributing, archiving, and revising the QM manual, QM procedural instructions, QM work instructions, and other quality assurance documents,
- ensuring compliance with the provisions of this manual by all employees,
- ordering, planning, and conducting internal audits and reporting the results to Management,
- informing Management about the respective quality status,
- documenting quality-relevant processes and archiving these records.

Within the scope of his role as QMR, Mr. Zenk is positioned organizationally so that he has direct access to SZMF Management at all times and is not subject to instructions from the areas to be audited.

In the event of disputes or differing interpretations regarding the application of the QMS with other areas and departments of SZMF-ES, SZMF Management shall decide as the higher-level authority.

The areas and departments of SZMF-ES are instructed to support Mr. Zenk in carrying out his work.
