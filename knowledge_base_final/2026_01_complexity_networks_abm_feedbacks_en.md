# A Complexity Primer — Networks, ABMs & Feedbacks

## Networks
- Key concepts: nodes, edges, degree, clustering, path length.
- Topologies matter: small-world, random, and power-law (scale-free) networks have distinct diffusion and fragility profiles.
- Recommendation: compute degree distribution, identify hubs, and design redundancy or protection for critical nodes.

## Agent‑Based Models (ABMs)
- ABMs model heterogeneous agents following local rules; useful for exploring mechanisms, counterfactuals and localized interventions.
- Use ABMs for evacuation models, epidemic scenarios, or any setting where space, heterogeneity and adaptation matter.

## Feedbacks & Path Dependence
- Positive feedback amplifies trends (can create lock-in); negative feedback stabilizes.
- Path dependence: early events can shape long-term trajectories; be mindful of incentives and early interventions.

## Practical tips
1. Start with toy models; scale up to ABMs only when necessary.
2. Use network diagnostics as part of routine monitoring.
3. Identify reinforcing loops and design policy levers to modulate them.
