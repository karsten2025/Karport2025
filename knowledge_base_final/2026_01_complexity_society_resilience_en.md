# A Complexity Primer — Rare Events, Uncertainty & Harnessing Complexity

## Rare events & long tails
- Complex systems often produce heavy‑tailed distributions where rare, large events coexist with many small events. Design for resilience, not just efficiency.

## Complexity vs Uncertainty
- Complexity can create apparent randomness; nonstationarity means past data may not predict future distributions. Prioritize adaptive policies and continuous measurement.

## Harnessing complexity — practical checklist
1. Diagnose: quick heuristics to decide if complexity is present.
2. Prototype & experiment: staged rollouts and measurement.
3. Monitor: leading indicators and early-warning signals (correlations, variance, critical slowing down).
4. Protect: keep slack, redundancy, and diversify supply/channels.
5. Govern: pre-defined response plans triggered by metrics; update continuously.

## Glossary (short)
- Emergence, Self‑organized criticality, Explore/Exploit, Rugged/Dancing landscape, Tipping point.

## FAQ (short)
Q: When to model? A: Model when heterogeneity, locality or adaptation matter; otherwise use simple heuristics.
Q: ABM vs aggregate model? A: Use aggregate for macro forecasts when heterogeneity is negligible; use ABM to explore mechanisms and interventions.
