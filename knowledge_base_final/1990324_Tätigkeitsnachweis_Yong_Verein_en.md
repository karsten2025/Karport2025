## YONG - Association for TAEKWON-DO and HAPKIDO Wolfenbüttel (registered association)

Wolfenbüttel, 24/03/1994

## Certificate of Activities for Mr. Karsten Zenk

This is to certify that Mr. Karsten Zenk, born 07/09/1967, residing at Am Roten Amte 2, 38302 Wolfenbüttel, has been active for the association in various areas of responsibility over the past five years.

His responsibilities as a coach and active athlete include supervising a children’s/youth group as well as leading a competitive performance group.

Mr. Zenk serves as the point of contact between other associations and the Yong Association.

In the organizational area, Mr. Zenk is and has been active on behalf of the Yong Association.

In his former role as cash auditor, he was responsible for the proper auditing of the accounts and records. Mr. Zenk currently represents the interests of the Yong Association as Managing Director.

In addition, Mr. Zenk is highly committed to the organization and delivery of association-related events.

(Frank Maller, Treasurer)
