# Methodological Map & Theoretical Foundation

Karsten Zenk pursues an interdisciplinary approach that is deeply rooted in Systems Theory, Constructivism, and Cognitive Science. This theoretical foundation enables him to manage projects not merely as technical sequences, but as complex adaptive systems.

## 1. Systems Theory & Cybernetics (Operational Complexity)

- **Focus:** Analysis of organizations as self-referential, autopoietic systems.
- **Key thinkers:** Niklas Luhmann, Dirk Baecker, Peter Fuchs, Helmut Willke, Fritz B. Simon.
- **Application:** Use of cybernetic principles (Varela, Maturana, Heinz von Förster) to optimize operational couplings and to leverage the intrinsic dynamics of systems for project success ("Harnessing Complexity").

## 2. Constructivism & Communication (Breaking Open Cognitive Closure)

- **Focus:** Radical and Social Constructivism as well as the pragmatics of communication.
- **Key thinkers:** Ernst von Glasersfeld, Paul Watzlawick, Berger & Luckmann.
- **Application:** Awareness of the subjectivity of reality constructions. Karsten uses this to identify situational cognitive closures among stakeholders and to open up new solution spaces through targeted communication.

## 3. Psychology, Cognition & Decision-Making

- **Focus:** Behavioral Economics, Transactional Analysis, and Affect Logic.
- **Key thinkers:** Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky (heuristics/biases), Eric Berne & Fanita English (Transactional Analysis), Luc Ciompi (Affect Logic).
- **Application:** Understanding human decision patterns and team dynamics. Reduction of dependencies through clear role understanding and the recognition of psychological "games".

## 4. Semantics & Diversity (Structure in Chaos)

- **Focus:** General Semantics and the power of diversity in systems.
- **Key thinkers:** Alfred Korzybski, S. I. Hayakawa ("The map is not the territory"), Scott E. Page.
- **Application:** Precision in language to avoid misunderstandings in high-performance teams; leveraging diversity as a tool for problem-solving in complex environments.

## 5. Mental Stance & Mindfulness (Mapping Suffer to Joy)

- **Focus:** Integration of Eastern and Western philosophies for mental stability.
- **Key thinkers:** Alan Watts, Eckhart Tolle.
- **Application:** Maintaining calm and overview (mindfulness) even during high-pressure phases of programs and portfolios. The aim is the transformation of operational stress (Suffer) into constructive value creation and joy (Joy).
