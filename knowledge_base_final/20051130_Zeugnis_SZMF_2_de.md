## Page 2 of the reference letter for Mr. Dipl.-Ing. Karsten Zenk dated 30 November 2005

Mr. Zenk has sound specialist knowledge and handled even difficult tasks confidently and independently. Furthermore, he possesses extensive interdisciplinary expertise, which he implemented excellently in his work. Issues of higher complexity were resolved very successfully by Mr. Zenk, among other things by making use of the training opportunities offered.

Mr. Zenk’s way of working was characterized by a high degree of independence, exemplary reliability, and a high level of commitment. He was resilient and met the specified deadlines even under exceptional workload. In doing so, Mr. Zenk consistently demonstrated a high degree of flexibility in his thinking and actions. Strong conceptual skills, very good analytical abilities, and a very quick grasp of new matters distinguished him.

His decisiveness and willingness to take on tasks were exemplary. Mr. Zenk was open, worked very cooperatively, and brought conflict situations to excellent results.

He consistently lived up to his leadership responsibility as a project manager and fulfilled the task assigned to him—leading team members in a goal-, performance-, and team-oriented manner—always to our fullest satisfaction. Mr. Zenk was at all times able to convince his employees and to implement suitable solutions.

His conduct toward superiors, colleagues, and employees was always exemplary. He was also highly valued by our customers, not least because of his courteous and professional manner.

Mr. Zenk is leaving our company today at his own request. We regret this decision greatly, as we are losing a valuable future management talent. We thank him for the trusting cooperation and wish him continued professional success and all the best personally.

Salzgitter, 30 November 2005

Salzgitter Mannesmann Forschung GmbH

Prof. Dr.-Ing. M. Niemeyer
