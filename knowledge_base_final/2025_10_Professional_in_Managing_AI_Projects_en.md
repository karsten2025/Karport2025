## PMI Certified Professional in Managing AI (PMI-CPMAI)™

Issued by <u>Project Management Institute</u>

The PMI-CPMAI™ certification assesses a professional’s ability to apply the CPMAI™ Methodology and manage AI initiatives from inception through implementation, addressing the unique challenges and requirements that distinguish AI projects from traditional software development initiatives. The PMI-CPMAI certification demonstrates competence in managing data-driven, iterative AI projects while ensuring responsible, ethical, and trustworthy AI implementation practices.

## **Earning Criteria**

- Completion of the 21-hour PMI-CPMAI Prep Course is required prior to scheduling the PMI-CPMAI certification exam.
- Successfully pass the 120-question PMI-CPMAI certification exam (2 hours and 40 minutes).
- Certification holders are required to earn and report 30 Professional Development Units (PDUs) within a three-year cycle and submit the certification renewal fee in order to maintain an active PMI-CPMAI credential
