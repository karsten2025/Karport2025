Detailed Methodological Map & Theoretical Foundation

Karsten Zenk pursues an interdisciplinary approach deeply rooted in Systems Theory, Constructivism, and Cognitive Science. This theoretical foundation enables him to manage projects not just as technical processes, but as complex adaptive systems.

1. Systems Theory & Cybernetics (Operational Complexity)

   Focus: Analysis of organizations as self-referential, autopoietic systems.

   Key Thinkers: Niklas Luhmann, Dirk Baecker, Peter Fuchs, Helmut Willke, Fritz B. Simon.

   Application: Using cybernetic principles (Varela, Maturana, Heinz von Förster) to optimize operational couplings and harness the inherent dynamics of systems for project success ("Harnessing Complexity").

2. Constructivism & Communication (Breaking Cognitive Closures)

   Focus: Radical and Social Constructivism and the pragmatics of communication.

   Key Thinkers: Ernst von Glasersfeld, Paul Watzlawick, Berger & Luckmann.

   Application: Awareness of the subjectivity of reality constructions. Karsten uses this to identify situational cognitive closures in stakeholders and open new solution spaces through targeted communication.

3. Psychology, Cognition & Decision Making

   Focus: Behavioral Economics, Transactional Analysis, and affect logic.

   Key Thinkers: Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky (Heuristics/Biases), Eric Berne & Fanita English (Transactional Analysis), Luc Ciompi (Affect Logic).

   Application: Understanding human decision patterns and dynamics in teams. Reduction of dependencies through clear role definitions and identifying psychological "games."

4. Semantics & Diversity (Structure in Chaos)

   Focus: General Semantics and the power of diversity in systems.

   Key Thinkers: Alfred Korzybski, S. I. Hayakawa ("The map is not the territory"), Scott E. Page.

   Application: Precision in language to avoid misunderstandings in high-performance teams; utilizing diversity as a tool for problem-solving in complex environments.

5. Mental Stance & Mindfulness (Mapping Suffer to Joy)

   Focus: Integration of Eastern and Western philosophies for mental stability.

   Key Thinkers: Alan Watts, Eckhart Tolle.

   Application: Maintaining composure and overview (mindfulness) even during high-pressure phases of programs and portfolios. The goal is to transform operational stress (suffer) into constructive value creation and joy.
